Last updated: October 6, 2026

6. Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. We retain information as reasonably necessary for orders, customer service, business records, security, and legal obligations.

Although we sell children's garments, our website is intended for parents, guardians, and other customers who place orders. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children for independent account creation or marketing.
